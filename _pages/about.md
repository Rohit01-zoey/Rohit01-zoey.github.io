I am a Research Collaborator advised by [Prof. Praneeth Vepakomma](https://sites.mit.edu/praneeth/) at MBZUAI. My current research broadly focuses on **misalignment (sabotage)** in LLMs and **scalable oversight**.

<h2 class="section-label"><span>Current directions</span></h2>

<div class="directions">
<p><b>Loss of Control in Multi-Agent Settings.</b><span>What new threat vectors do multi-agent settings expose, and can current monitoring techniques keep up?</span></p>
<p><b>Social Dynamics in Agent Swarms.</b><span>How do agent swarms organize themselves, what threats arise, and how do these systems compare to current paradigms (individual models or small swarms)?</span></p>
<p><b>Chain-of-Thought (CoT) Controllability &amp; Obfuscation in LLMs.</b><span>To what extent can LLMs control and conceal their CoT reasoning?</span></p>
</div>

<h2 class="section-label"><span>Background</span></h2>

<p class="bg-short">Previously: parameter-efficient fine-tuning, federated learning for LLMs, (multimodal) reasoning, and LLMs in healthcare. M.S. in Computer Science at Duke University; B.Tech &amp; M.Tech at IIT Bombay.</p>

<details class="bg-more" markdown="1">
<summary><span class="show">More &darr;</span><span class="hide">Less &uarr;</span></summary>

My previous work spans several areas, including:

- _Parameter-efficient fine-tuning_, improving expressivity and adaptability of large models
- _Federated learning for LLMs_, with an emphasis on communication-efficient training
- _(Multimodal) reasoning_, studying how models process and reason over visual and textual inputs
- LLM applications in healthcare, focusing on real-world user interaction with medical chatbots

Prior to this, I completed my M.S. in Computer Science at Duke University, where I worked with [Prof. Bhuwan Dhingra](https://users.cs.duke.edu/~bdhingra/) on the mathematical reasoning capabilities of multimodal LLMs.

During this time, I also interned with [Prof. Praneeth Vepakomma](https://sites.mit.edu/praneeth/) at MBZUAI (Summer 2025), working on efficient and scalable training methods for large models, and collaborated with [Prof. Monica Agrawal](https://www.monicaagrawal.com/) on medical chatbots and their real-world usability.

Before this, I completed my B.Tech in Electrical Engineering and M.Tech in Artificial Intelligence and Data Science at IIT Bombay.
